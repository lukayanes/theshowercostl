OUR STORY ›

A FAMILY BUSINESS
BUILT ON EXPERIENCE

The Shower Company is a family-owned business with more than 25 years of experience specializing in bathroom remodeling. Our team focuses on building bathrooms that are functional, beautiful, and designed to last.

You might wonder how a family business grew from its first location in Florida to serving homeowners in Kansas City and St. Louis. The answer is simple: commitment, hard work, and a willingness to take risks while always putting customers first. That dedication is the foundation of the King family’s story.

Our mission is to eliminate the stress and uncertainty that often comes with bathroom renovations. We focus on delivering a smooth experience from the first consultation through installation, with thoughtful design and quality craftsmanship guiding every project.

We offer high-quality one-day shower and bathtub remodeling systems designed with you, fabricated for your space, and installed by our experienced team. If you'd like to learn more about the renovation process, explore what to expect during your project.

OUR PROCESS ›

WHAT TO EXPECT
FROM START TO FINISH

1

Free In-Home Consultation

We start by visiting your home to take measurements, gather photos, and walk through your vision in detail. This allows us to fully understand your space and goals before any work begins.

2

Quote and Approval

After your consultation, we provide a clear, detailed quote with no hidden fees. Once approved, we move forward with scheduling and ordering your materials.

3

Ordering and Scheduling

All materials are ordered specifically for your project. We then schedule your installation and assign a dedicated crew who stays on your job until it is complete.

4

Installation and Completion

Our installers focus solely on your bathroom — no bouncing between jobs. Most remodels are completed within four to six weeks from your initial call. The price we quote is the price you pay.
