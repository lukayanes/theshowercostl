FAQ ›

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

Who does your installations?

All installations are completed by experienced Shower Company employees. We do not rely on outside subcontractors, which allows us to maintain consistent quality, training, and accountability on every project.

Is this company a franchise?

No. The Shower Company is a family-owned business. Our team operates locally and focuses on delivering high-quality bathroom remodeling with experienced installers and direct communication throughout the project.

How long will the project take?

Many shower and bathtub installations can be completed in as little as one day depending on the system being installed. Larger bathroom renovations may take longer depending on materials, layout changes, and structural work required.

Do you do other projects around the house?

Our team focuses specifically on bathroom remodeling. By specializing in showers, bathtubs, and full bathroom renovations, we’re able to provide higher quality work and a more efficient process for homeowners.

What is the average cost of a shower remodel?

Shower remodel costs vary depending on materials, glass enclosures, plumbing adjustments, and installation complexity. During your consultation we provide a clear estimate based on your design choices and the condition of your existing bathroom.

Are bathtub liners any good?

Bathtub liners can be a quick cosmetic solution, but they often trap moisture and may not address underlying issues. Many homeowners choose a full tub replacement instead to ensure proper drainage, waterproofing, and long-term durability.

Who will be working in my home?

Your project will be completed by trained Shower Company installers. Because we do not rely on subcontractors, you’ll have a consistent team handling the entire installation from preparation to completion.

What type of warranty do we offer?

Warranty coverage depends on the materials used in your project and the manufacturer providing them. Our team will review available warranties with you during the consultation so you understand exactly what is covered.

Do we resurface?

No. The Shower Company focuses on full shower and bathtub remodeling rather than resurfacing. This approach allows us to rebuild the space correctly with new materials designed for long-term performance.
