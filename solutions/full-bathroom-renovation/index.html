FAQ ›

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

What is a realistic budget for a bathroom remodel?

Bathroom remodel budgets vary depending on materials, layout changes, and the condition of the existing space. Smaller updates cost less, while full rebuilds with plumbing or structural work require a larger investment.

What is the most expensive part of a bathroom remodel?

The wet area is typically the most expensive portion of a remodel. Showers, waterproofing systems, tile work, and plumbing upgrades often account for the largest part of the project cost.

What adds the most value to a bathroom?

Updated showers, modern fixtures, improved lighting, and durable materials tend to provide the greatest return on investment. Clean layouts and high-quality installations also increase resale value.

What flooring is best for a bathroom?

Waterproof materials such as porcelain tile, luxury vinyl, and stone are common choices because they resist moisture and hold up well in high-humidity environments.

What is the best time to renovate a bathroom?

Bathroom renovations can be completed year-round. Many homeowners schedule projects during slower seasons so work can begin sooner and material availability is better.

Is it worth hiring a professional remodeler?

Professional remodelers bring experience with waterproofing, plumbing, ventilation, and building codes. This helps prevent costly issues and ensures the project is completed safely and correctly.

What is the average lifespan of a bathroom?

Most bathrooms last between 15 and 25 years depending on materials and maintenance. Upgrading older systems can improve durability, efficiency, and comfort.

What’s trending in bathroom remodels?

Walk-in showers, large format tile, modern lighting, floating vanities, and improved storage solutions are some of the most popular trends homeowners are choosing today.

What are common hidden bathroom costs?

Hidden costs often come from water damage, plumbing updates, ventilation improvements, or structural repairs discovered after demolition begins.

In what order should I redo my bathroom?

The correct order is structure first: plumbing, waterproofing, and ventilation. After that comes flooring, wall systems, fixtures, lighting, and final finishes.
